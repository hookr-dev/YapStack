The YapStack **source code** is free software under [`AGPL-3.0-only`](LICENSE). The
**"YapStack" name, logo, and associated marks** are a separate matter: the code is
free, but the name identifies *our* project and *our* hosted service. This policy
follows the well-worn Ghost / Plausible model — it protects users from confusion
without restricting the software freedoms the AGPL grants.

## You may, without asking

- **Run YapStack for yourself or your organization**, self-hosted, at any scale,
  including on your own subdomains, using the YapStack name to describe what you are
  running ("I self-host YapStack").
- **Modify and redistribute the source** under the AGPL, including patched builds for
  your own use.
- **Refer to YapStack** accurately in articles, tutorials, comparisons, talks, and
  compatibility statements ("works with YapStack", "a YapStack client").
- **Contribute** upstream under the [`DCO`](DCO).

## You must rebrand if you

- **Distribute a modified fork as a product** under the YapStack name, or under a
  name/logo confusingly similar to it. Forks are welcome — the AGPL guarantees them —
  but a fork is **your** project and must carry **your** name, so users are not misled
  about who stands behind it.
- Use the YapStack marks in a way that implies **endorsement, affiliation, or
  official status** that does not exist.

## You need our written permission to

- **Offer a commercial hosted YapStack service** (SaaS resale of the software as a
  paid managed service) using the **YapStack name or marks**. The software freedom to
  run a hosted service is fully granted by the AGPL; this restriction is on using
  **our brand** to sell it. (Reimplementing the private control-plane admin contract
  to self-serve is explicitly fine — see LICENSING.md — it just can't trade on our
  name.)
- Use the YapStack marks in a **domain name, company name, or product name** for a
  commercial offering.

## Spirit

The dividing line is **brand confusion, not code freedom**. Self-hosting, forking,
studying, patching, and describing YapStack are always free. Selling a hosted service
*under our name* is what needs a conversation. When in doubt, ask —
`trademark@` the project domain.
